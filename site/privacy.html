The Vassy app

Vassy runs on your Mac. It has no account and no telemetry, and it sends us nothing about you or your sessions.

  • Its state (which sessions are waiting, their summaries, your settings) is stored in the folder ~/.vassy on your Mac.
  • To write a summary, Vassy hands an excerpt of a session's transcript to the Claude Code or Codex command-line tool that is already installed and signed in on your Mac. That tool sends it to its provider (Anthropic or OpenAI) under your own account and that provider's terms. We are not involved in this and cannot see it.
  • Updates: every few hours the app requests the file vassy.app/appcast.xml to see whether a newer version exists. If one does, it downloads it from vassy.app, verifies its signature and installs it. These requests carry no identifier and no cookies, only the app’s name and version; as with any visit to this website, the hosting provider processes your IP address to answer them (see above). You can switch this off in the app’s gear menu under “Update automatically”.
  • macOS may contact Apple to check the app's notarization when you first open it. That is a function of macOS, not of Vassy.
